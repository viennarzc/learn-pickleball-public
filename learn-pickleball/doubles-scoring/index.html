How does doubles pickleball scoring work?

A standard doubles score call has three numbers: the serving team’s score, the receiving team’s score, then the server number.

Rule baseline: GPF · Reviewed 1 Sep 2026

Example

4 – 2 – 2

Serving team: 4 · Receiving team: 2 · Second server

Say the numbers in this order

01

Your team’s score. Begin with the score of the team that is about to serve.

02

The other team’s score. Name the receiving team’s score second.

03

The server number. In doubles, say whether the first or second server is serving.

Why say it before the serve?

Calling the score before each serve gives all four players the same reference point. It reduces confusion about the score, the serving order, and whether a side-out has happened.

Quick practice: if the serving team has 4, the receiving team has 2, and the second partner serves, call “4 – 2 – 2.”

Recreational games can sound different

Casual groups may use local shorthand or help one another remember a score. That is a social convention, not a replacement for the official format. Agree on the approach before starting your game.
