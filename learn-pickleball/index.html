01 · COURT RULE

What is the kitchen in pickleball?

Standing in the non-volley zone is allowed. Volleys there—and momentum that carries you in after one—are the problem.

02 · SERVING

Where does a pickleball serve go?

Start behind the baseline and send the ball diagonally into the opposite service court, beyond the kitchen line.

03 · RALLY RULE

What is the two-bounce rule?

The serve and return each bounce before either side may volley, giving the opening rally a clear rhythm.

How does doubles scoring work?

A common doubles call names the serving score, receiving score, then the server number.
