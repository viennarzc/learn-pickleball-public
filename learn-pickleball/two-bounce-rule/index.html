What is the two-bounce rule in pickleball?

At the start of every rally, the serve must bounce and the return must bounce before either team may volley.

Rule baseline: GPF and USA Pickleball · Reviewed 1 Sep 2026

The order is the rule

01

Serve bounces. The serve lands in the receiver’s diagonal service court.

02

Return bounces. The return lands on the serving team’s side before they play it.

03

The rally opens. Once both required bounces have happened, volleys can be part of play.

Why it matters

Without this opening sequence, a team could serve and rush to the net for an immediate volley. The two-bounce rule gives the returner and server a more even first exchange.

Say it out loud: serve, bounce, return, bounce—then play.

Do not mix it up with the kitchen

The two-bounce rule applies to the start of a rally. The kitchen rule applies to volleys near the net throughout a rally. Both rules can matter in the same point, but they answer different questions.
