Where does a pickleball serve go?

A pickleball serve travels diagonally over the net into the opposite service court. Think: across the net, then across the centre line.

Rule baseline: GPF · Reviewed 1 Sep 2026

Three landmarks make the target clear

01

Start behind the baseline. The baseline is your first court landmark.

02

Look diagonally across the court. Your target is not straight ahead.

03

Clear the kitchen line. The serve must land in the receiving service court, beyond the non-volley zone.

One easy mental picture

Imagine a line from the lower-left corner of the court to the upper-right service court. That diagonal is your target. From the lower-right side, the target switches to the upper-left service court.

Before you serve: check your starting side, then trace the diagonal—not a straight line.
