Learn/Gear

Beginner pickleball gear guide

For your first game, start with a paddle you can comfortably swing, a ball that suits the court, and shoes you can move sideways in. Ask your host what you can borrow before you shop.

Borrow a paddle first

Ask the venue or group about loaners. Try a few easy swings and short blocks at the net. Look for a handle you can hold without squeezing and a paddle you can bring back to ready position comfortably.

If you’re buying, use what you noticed with the loaner to compare grip size and how the paddle feels in motion. Check the return policy if you’re ordering online. A second paddle and a premium upgrade can wait until you’ve played a few times.

Match the ball to the court

Ask what ball your group uses and whether they supply it. Indoor and outdoor balls can feel different, and the playing surface matters. A covered court with an outdoor-style surface may use the same ball as an outdoor court.

If you need to bring your own, start with a small pack of the type your group recommends. Save the bulk pack until you know where you’ll play regularly.

Wear court shoes

If you already have court shoes suited to the surface, start there. Check your venue’s sole rules, especially on indoor floors that require non-marking shoes.

When trying on a pair, wear the socks you play in. Check that your heel stays in place and that you have toe room when you stop and step sideways.

First-game checklist

  • Confirm the court surface and any footwear rules.
  • Reserve a loaner paddle or pack your own.
  • Ask whether balls are provided and bring the recommended type if needed.
  • Pack court shoes and water. A gear bag and spare accessories can wait.

Playing in an event?

Ask the organizer which equipment rules apply. For events that use USA Pickleball rules, check the exact paddle model and ball on the official equipment list.
